   Merge review expectations:
    - Be constructive and respectful when giving feedback
    - Check for code quality

7. How will we handle conflicts (i.e. differing opinions)? Note: If conflicts cannot resolved this way, please contact your tutor.
   Conflicting team members will first discuss the issue directly. All conflicting opinions/ideas will be discuss in a respectful 
   and civil manner. If the issue remains unresolved, a neutral third party member will mediate the conflict to ensure fairness and 
   encourage compromise. Ideally all conflicts will be resolved by coming to a collective consensus. If the issue still remains unresolved,
   the tutor will be contacted. Throughout the process, all members commit to open, respectful, and solution-focused communication.

8. List the steps a team member does if they get stuck (e.g. stuck debugging).
   1. Review the provided resources (e.g. lecture slides, notes, any exemplar code)
   2. Consult with another team member for pair debugging or advice/suggestions
   3. Check the online forum 
   4. Ask tutor during lab hours or send them a messsage if urgent

9. A day before the due date we notice a team member's work is very broken and incomplete. Instead of waiting until the due date, list the steps we will take to prevent this from occuring in the first place. Note: contact your tutor if this happens as well.
   1. Check on with the team member with compassion and kindness to investigate their personal situation
   2. Conduct a group meeting to reallocate/redistribute the team member's iternary
   3. Notify tutor of issue and the steps the team are taking to address said issue

   In addition, to prevent this situation from occuring, the team will conduct checkups on every member's progress 2 days before the due date. 
   All team members will be transparent on their progress with their assigned task, promptly seeking help from other team members if needed. 
   If the issue risks affecting overall project quality or deadlines, the tutor will be informed promptly.
